About Melody

Melody Jones is a candidate for the House of Representatives in District 31. She has lived in West Valley for approximately 29 ½ years with her family of 6 children. She was born in Idaho; her family moved to New Mexico, Arizona, and Utah. She has extensive firsthand experience.

As an independent cosmetologist for the past 30 years, she has an understanding of the challenges faced by entrepreneurs, individuals, and families during difficult financial times. She has operated her business from two salons that have often required lengthy hours to make ends meet. She listens to her clients and knows what’s really important, and her clients discover her compassion and common-sense approach to provide real and substantive solutions.

Melody Jones has served as a state delegate, county delegate, and precinct chair. Her focus for the future is her family, giving to the community through service, and giving time and attention to the needs of District 31 and the state of Utah.

Her priorities include empowering economic growth, limiting taxation, supporting creation and innovation for small businesses, and inspiring a larger vision involving more opportunities for all Utahns. She wants Utah to be a light for the world to emulate.
